# Architecture Decision Log

Copy this template for every important decision. Judges may ask why a design was chosen.

## ADR Template

### Title

### Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

### Context

What requirement, risk, or constraint created the decision?

### Decision

What exactly will the team do?

### Alternatives considered

- Option A.
- Option B.

### Consequences

- Benefits.
- Costs.
- Risks.
- Reversal strategy.
